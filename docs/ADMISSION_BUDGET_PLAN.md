# Admission groups, application budget and time planning

## Done conditions and scope

Every supported system (US, UK, HK, CA, AU, SG) has Reach / Target / Safety planning columns. International grouping uses the student's explicit choice or an exact course/qualification comparison; unknown evidence stays visible rather than filling quotas. Financial affordability is separate. The budget derives from the owned My list, keeps currencies/years/routes explicit, preserves missing costs, deduplicates shared platforms, compares alternative enrolment scenarios, and incorporates actual essay workload plus explicit extra work. Data must remain owned, encrypted, exportable and deletable. Relevant automated and real packaged-app browser checks plus desktop releases/local upgrade validate delivery.

This is a planning tool, not a complete worldwide course, cost or admission-probability database. The initial public entry-reference extract is deliberately small; user-confirmed records cover other courses. Source URLs must be on the selected institution's domain. A URL and student confirmation do not represent independent TrueDraft certification. Programme entry dates, fee status, qualification, subject conditions and route must all be checked.

## International groups

`programmeFit` in `src/lib/student-resources.ts` compares only the selected field, configured programme, entry year and matching qualification scale. IB is out of45; ATAR out of99.95. Three UK/international A-levels use A*=6,A=5,B=4,C=3,D=2,E=1 as a planning index; it is not a qualification conversion, and all individual subject/grade conditions must separately be confirmed. Different percentage curricula/averaging methods cannot be compared without the same named qualification. Unsupported scales remain unassessed.

- Below a published requirement is flagged as potentially ineligible, rather than a viable low-probability option.
- Highly competitive courses or required outstanding assessment remain Reach.
- A minimum eligibility floor cannot establish Target/Safety.
- A typical-offer/admitted band can suggest Target only when subjects, English, applicant audience and selection conditions are checked.
- Provisional Safety uses explicit planning headroom above the recorded upper requirement:3 IB points,2 three-A-level points,8 percentage points or5 ATAR points; or an applicable published guarantee whose exact route is confirmed. This heuristic is transparent and is not a fitted admission-probability model.
- Domestic-only guarantees never apply to international applicants. Applicant status is entered independently for each system; a generic domestic status in one country is not reused in another.
- A student may override their final list category. Its origin is labeled student-selected, separate from computed suggestions. No grade, budget, income or financial record is added to an AI request. AI preserves server-supplied categories.

Primary entry references include [Cambridge2027 computing](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/computer-science-ba-hons-meng), [Bath2027 computing](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/computer-science/bsc-computer-science/), [Manchester2027 computing](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses/2027/00560/bsc-computer-science/), [UBC general IB eligibility](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/admissions/applicants-international-baccalaureate-and-advanced-placement-courses), [HKU2027/28 computing booklet](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2026-10/CDS%20Booklet.pdf), [NUS IB requirements](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/ib-diploma/admission-requirements) and [UQ2027 domestic ATAR guarantee](https://study.uq.edu.au/admissions/undergraduate/review-entry-requirements/guaranteed-atar). UBC's general24-point minimum is not a competitive CS cutoff; NUS's Singapore A-level representative profiles are not an IB cutoff; UQ explicitly excludes student-visa applicants from this guarantee. Reference conditions are not silently checked for the student.

## Budget workflow

`/my-list/resources` is linked from My list, recommendations and the timetable. Default display and household input currency are USD (user requested). These are separate units: changing display currency does not reinterpret the household budget. Original school amounts retain their own bill currency. Supported currencies:USD,CNY,GBP,HKD,CAD,AUD,SGD,EUR.

Students enter application-season budget, annual family contribution, available arrival cash, aid need and contingency. Each school records fee status, route, programme-choice count, degree years, annual escalation and year-round living coverage. Every cost line can be unknown, estimated, student-confirmed, paid, inapplicable or approved-waived, with amount/range, source, year, net payment, waiver-pending state and scope notes. Pending waivers are not deducted. Missing amounts/rates appear as incomplete known subtotals, never zero-cost claims.

Application fees across all choices are added. Shared fees are charged once per configured platform and cycle:

- [UCAS2027](https://www.ucas.com/faqs/what-is-the-application-fee-for-the-2027-cycle):GBP34.50 for up to five standard undergraduate choices, with choice-limit and [usual Oxford/Cambridge restriction](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/filling-in-your-ucas-application) warnings. Other cycles and university extras require separate evidence.
- [OUAC2027](https://www.ouac.on.ca/guide/undergrad-fees):CAD159 for three programme choices, CAD51 each thereafter. University-specific programme/document evaluation and any payment/service fees are separate. No obsolete international-service charge is copied into2027. One school can contain multiple programme choices; the usual three-per-university limit is flagged.
- JUPAS,UAC,QTAC,VTAC and other shared routes can be configured with their actual cycle, currency, fee and choice limit. An unconfigured route remains incomplete. UCAS/OUAC prices are not applied to those routes.

Shared tests/retakes, score reports after free allocations, translations/certification, courier, portfolios, auditions/interviews, optional counselling and other expenses use actual quantities. Students explicitly review applicability before totals are considered complete. Paid application costs are included in total spending, not discounted; paid sunk costs survive withdrawal/denial while unneeded future charges stop. Payments are net of any refund the student records.

Attendance costs are **alternative scenarios**, never the sum of tuition at all chosen universities. Each includes gross annual tuition, compulsory fees, housing, food, transport, books, insurance and personal costs, plus arrival visa/residence, whole-term health surcharge, travel and setup. Annual extras can include flights/vacations, payment/loan costs and other recurring needs. Vacation coverage and actual degree duration must be confirmed. Inflation is an editable assumption, not a fee forecast; FX snapshots are held constant and contingency is explicit.

Only confirmed awarded grants reduce net costs, for their confirmed duration. Hoped-for scholarships, loans and future employment do not reduce costs. Loans remain potential debt. Credited or fully refundable deposits are cash requirements rather than duplicate net expenses; noncredited/nonrefundable amounts are added. Proof-of-funds is shown separately and not added again as an expense. Cash requirements, first-year cost, annual net/gap and whole-degree estimate remain separate. Preliminary financial-fit labeling requires complete checked current-cycle amounts, the right fee status, year-round living coverage, household budget and sufficient pre-arrival cash; a partial estimate cannot certify financial safety.

Initial amount references include CMU's [2026–27 resident first-year schedule](https://www.cmu.edu/sfs/tuition/undergraduate/index.html), SCU's [2026–27 fee schedule](https://www.scu.edu/media/offices/bursar/2026-27-Tuition-%26-Student-Fees-Schedule-UG-and-Graduate-Programs--Apr-2026.pdf), [HKU2027 application fee](https://www.admissions.hku.hk/node/172), [NUS application overview](https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/application-overview) and [NUS living-cost guide](https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/financial-aid/living-costs). Historical US CDS/out-of-state federal tuition is a labeled reference, not a current international quote. CMU's one-time first-year fee is kept out of recurring annual fees. NUS's academic-year living guide excludes vacations and housing; its international application fee excludes Music. Unverified costs stay missing.

The optional FX button fetches the fixed public [ECB XML endpoint](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml), validates all required positive unit-per-EUR rates and date, and saves a dated snapshot. Currency conversion divides by the source rate and multiplies by the target rate. Bank/card charges and future FX movements remain separate. No applicant data is sent. Client PUT cannot replace the server-stored rate snapshot. Offline source-currency calculations still work; missing cross-rates remain unknown.

## Time and privacy

Remaining/scheduled/unplaced active hours derive from actual selected supplements, essays, progress and list status in the existing planner. Shared statements are counted once. Additional work tasks can be shared or school-linked with active hours, target date and external processing lead days; lead days move the suggested request date and are not counted as work hours. Arrival tasks need admission status and an explicit target date. Extra completion uses the same owned planner completion store, including undo. Unknown dates, past request dates, over-capacity and unplaced work remain visible.

The new resource object is stored inside the existing AES-GCM-encrypted application schedule, with backward-compatible defaults and no database migration. API authentication/ownership and duplicate/source validation apply. Updating ordinary timetable preferences preserves resource data. Account export includes it and deletion cascades it. No invoices, payment credentials or external financial accounts are connected. The feature does not make payments or submit applications.
